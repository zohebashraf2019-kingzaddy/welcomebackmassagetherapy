Meet Ellie

A quiet room downtown,
built to help you land.

Welcome Back Massage Therapy is a professional massage studio in downtown Reno. Ellie Del Chiaro, LMT, blends Swedish and deep tissue techniques to ease pain, melt stress, and bring you back into balance.

Every session starts by listening: where it hurts, what your days look like, how much pressure feels good. From there it's warm towels, low light, and hands that know what they're doing.

The studio lives inside Stardust Reno, a shared space of independent wellness pros (with a sauna down the hall).
